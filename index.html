    Every time you open Hi+OPTIMIZER, it stops and asks two things first. That's not buried in a settings page somewhere — it's the first thing you see.

    Before you run anything as Administrator

    Is this safe to run on a laptop?

    Most modules are fine. The Power Tweaks module specifically disables sleep, hibernation, and fast startup, and is flagged "not recommended for laptops" in the tool itself — skip that one on battery‑powered devices.

    What's the actual difference between Free and Paid?

    Free gives you 8 modules with basic tweaks — CPU, GPU, RAM, Network, Latency, System/Perf, Debloat, and Wifi Fix. It's fine to try things out, but it's the light version. Paid gives you the full set of high-impact system tweaks on every one of those modules, plus three extra modules free doesn't have at all: Disk Tweaks, Game Booster, and Power Tweaks. If you actually want your PC to feel different, paid is the one to get — and it's a one-time $5, with lifetime updates and support included.

    Can I undo everything afterward?

    Yes — restore the System Restore checkpoint from launch, or re‑import the .reg backups from C:\RegistryBackup. Any "Disable" module also has a matching "Re‑enable" entry in the same menu.

    Does it need an internet connection?

    No — every module runs local registry, service, and power‑plan commands. Nothing is downloaded or phoned home.
